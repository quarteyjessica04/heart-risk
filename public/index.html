    About this app

    CardioRisk estimates the probability of heart disease from ten health indicators using a logistic-regression model trained on the supplied hospital dataset. Predictions run entirely on your device – no patient data is sent anywhere, and the app works offline once installed.

    Important

    This is an educational/demo tool and not a medical device. It must not be used to diagnose, treat or make clinical decisions. Always consult a qualified clinician.

    Retraining

    Run python ml/train.py your_data.csv to retrain. The performance tab and predictions update automatically after redeploying.
